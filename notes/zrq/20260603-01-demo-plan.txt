The features I want to add are metrics and costs.
* Metrics describe benchmark measurements of the capabilities or performance of the platform.
  e.g. a numeric representation of the compute performance or IO speed of the platform.
* Costs describe the expected costs of performing a task.
  Costs can be anything from an actual monetary cost for using the platform to an estimate
  of the energy use or carbon cost of performing a task.

The costs and metrics would apply to all of the components in the data model.
In terms of the schema, this means adding a list of metrics and a list of costs
to both ExecutionRequest and AbstractComponent.

Where possible the new schema elements should follow the patterns
established in the rest of the schema.

The following code blocks outline the things I am thinking of.
